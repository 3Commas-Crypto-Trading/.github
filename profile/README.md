## 🧠 Overview

**3Commas** is an advanced cryptocurrency trading platform that allows users to automate strategies, manage portfolios, and execute trades across numerous crypto exchanges from a single professional workspace. It combines intelligent trading tools, automation, and portfolio analytics to simplify both active trading and long-term investment management.
The platform includes SmartTrade, DCA and Grid bots, portfolio tracking, backtesting, customizable alerts, and AI-assisted strategy creation. With support for multiple exchanges and cloud-based automation, traders can monitor markets and execute strategies around the clock without keeping their computer online. 

Designed for crypto investors, algorithmic traders, swing traders, portfolio managers, market analysts, professionals, and Windows users.
